Third-party software included in the OpenRip installer
=======================================================

FFmpeg (ffmpeg.exe, ffprobe.exe)
  Build: BtbN FFmpeg-Builds, "win64-gpl" variant.
  License: GNU GPL v3 (this build includes GPL components such as x264 and x265).
  Source code and build scripts: https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds
  FFmpeg source: https://ffmpeg.org/download.html
  OpenRip runs ffmpeg.exe and ffprobe.exe as separate programs.

libbluray (libbluray-*.dll) and libaacs (libaacs.dll)
  License: GNU LGPL v2.1
  Source: https://code.videolan.org/videolan/libbluray
          https://code.videolan.org/videolan/libaacs
  Built by the MSYS2 project: https://www.msys2.org/
  The DLLs they depend on (for example libgcrypt and libxml2) come from MSYS2 as well;
  their license texts are in this "licenses" folder.

OpenRip itself is available under the MIT or Apache-2.0 license.
