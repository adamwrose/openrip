OpenRip is installed, with FFmpeg and the Blu-ray libraries (libbluray and libaacs) included.

Copy-protected Blu-rays also need a key file, which is not included and which you must get
yourself from online. Save it as:

    %APPDATA%\aacs\KEYDB.cfg

(The installer created that folder for you.) Discs without copy protection, and DVDs, work
without it.

Only rip discs you own, and check the laws in your country about copy protection.
